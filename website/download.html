Get Tequila.

Free for Windows, Mac and Linux. No account, no licence key, nothing to sign up for.

Version 2026.11.01.1340, released 1 November 2026 · What's new



Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. An NVIDIA card (GTX 900 series or newer) or an AMD card (RX 5000 series or newer) renders on the graphics card, every card at once; any other computer renders on the processor.

Installer

Tequila-Setup-<version>.exe



Mac

A Mac with Apple's own chips (M1 or newer) and a recent macOS. Renders with Cycles on the Mac's graphics.

Disk image

Tequila-<version>.dmg



Intel Mac

A Mac with an Intel processor and macOS 12 or newer. Renders with Tequila's own renderer: Cycles comes from Blender, whose current version no longer runs on Intel Macs.

Disk image

Tequila-<version>-intel.dmg



Linux

64-bit Linux with glibc 2.35 or newer: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or later. NVIDIA and AMD cards render with Cycles.

Archive

Tequila-<version>-linux-x86_64.tar.gz

Every version, with its SHA-256 checksums, is on the Releases page.

The first time you open it

Your computer warns you, because Tequila isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.

  • Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Mac says Apple could not verify it: click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Tequila.

Installing and updating

  • Windows: run the installer. To update, run a newer one over it.
  • Mac: open the disk image and drag Tequila into Applications.
  • Linux: unpack the archive anywhere and run ./Tequila. ./install.sh adds it to the application menu.
  • Updates: Help › Check for updates fetches the newest version from GitHub, checks it against its SHA-256 and installs it. Your materials, plugins and settings are kept.

From source

Build it yourself.

Tequila is written in Rust. You need the Rust toolchain and two prebuilt SDKs, Intel Embree and Open Image Denoise (both Apache-2.0), which a script fetches.

# Windows
pwsh scripts/fetch-third-party.ps1
# macOS and Linux
bash scripts/fetch-third-party.sh

cargo run --release -p tequila-app

That runs Tequila with its own renderer. Cycles and the OpenCASCADE STEP reader are built separately; the README has the details.
