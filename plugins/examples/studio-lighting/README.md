# Studio Lighting

21 ready-made product-photography studios for Tequila. One click sets the
lights, backdrop, floor and colour grade around your camera; your materials
and your camera are never touched. Then fine-tune any studio: key, fill and
rims, how hard or soft the light is, warmth, gel colours, backdrop colour and
glow, floor and reflections.

## Turning it on

Studio Lighting comes with Tequila.

1. **Plugins → Manage plugins…**, and tick **Studio Lighting** on. Tequila
   asks first, because the plugin runs Python (3.9 or newer, from
   python.org).
2. **Plugins → Studio Lighting → Studio Lighting…** opens the main panel.

## Using it

Frame your product with the camera first, then pick a studio and click
**Apply**. The lights are placed round the camera as it is at that moment,
so the key light lands on the side the studio was designed for. Orbit to a
new angle? Click **Apply** again.

- **Ctrl+Z** undoes one Apply. **Back to my look** puts back everything as
  it was before the first studio, however many you tried.
- **Next studio ▶** steps through all 21 with your fine-tuning kept.
- Fine-tuning carries over from studio to studio and is kept between
  sessions, until you reset it.

## The three panels

### Studio Lighting (Plugins → Studio Lighting → Studio Lighting…)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Studio** | Which studio to apply. What it's for is shown under it after Apply. |
| **Turn the lights °** | Swings the whole rig round the product. 0 = as designed, for your camera. |
| **Brightness (stops)** | +1 is twice as bright, −1 half. Exposure only: the balance between the lights stays. |
| **Mood %** | How strongly the studio's contrast, vignette, glow and colour grade apply. 0 = neutral, 100 = as designed, 200 = pushed. |

### Lights (Plugins → Studio Lighting → Lights…)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Key light %** | The main light: it shapes the product and casts its shadow. |
| **Key size %** | Smaller = harder light (crisp shadows, sharp highlights); larger = softer, wrapping light. Its brightness stays the same. |
| **Key height °** | Raise it for an overhead studio feel, lower it for long, dramatic side light. |
| **Fill %** | Lifts the shadows, with the room's ambient light. 0 = deep shadows; more = flatter, cleaner light. |
| **Rims %** | Edge lights behind the product that outline it against the backdrop. |
| **Accents %** | The extras some studios add: sparkle lights for gems, halos, glows behind glass. |
| **Warmth** | Tints the white lights: minus is cooler (blue), plus warmer (amber). Coloured gels keep their colour. |
| **Mirror left ↔ right** | Puts the key light, and everything else, on the other side. |
| **Use my gel colours, Gel A, Gel B** | Recolours the coloured lights of Cinematic Teal & Orange and Split Gel. A is the front or left gel, B the back or right one. |

### Backdrop & floor (Plugins → Studio Lighting → Backdrop & floor…)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Backdrop** | What's seen behind the product: as the studio, black, charcoal, greys, white, a custom colour, or **My environment (HDRI)** - your own HDRI stays behind and in the reflections, and only the studio's lights are added. |
| **Custom colour** | Used when Backdrop is Custom colour. The floor's shadows take its hue, like a coloured paper sweep. |
| **Backdrop glow %** | The pool of light on the backdrop straight behind the product. 0 = an even backdrop. |
| **Floor** | As the studio, Shadows only (a seamless sweep), Satin or Glossy (soft or sharp reflections), Black mirror (a polished black table), or No floor (the product floats). |
| **Reflection %** | How strongly the floor reflects the product. |
| **Floor shadows** | The product's shadow on the floor. Off for a clean cut-out look. |
| **Place the lights around my camera** | On: the key is always on the same side of the picture. Off: the studio stays put in the scene, so orbiting shows it from other sides. |

## The studios

**Clean and commercial**

| Studio | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Three-Point Classic | A soft key 45° front-left, a gentle fill opposite and a rim behind, on a soft grey backdrop with a glow. | Anything - start here. |
| White Infinity | Pure white background, big soft lights above and both sides, a light contact shadow. | E-commerce, catalogues, marketplaces. |
| Soft Light Tent | Light from every side, like inside a diffusion tent: almost no shadows, smooth reflections. | Glossy and reflective products, cosmetics, ceramics. |
| Clamshell Beauty | A beauty dish high in front and a reflector below: even, flattering light, round catchlights, soft rims. | Cosmetics, skincare, fragrance, packaging. |
| Overhead Flat-Lay | One big softbox straight overhead, soft strips either side: short, soft shadows. | Top-down shots and tabletop arrangements. |
| Ring Light | A ring of light round the lens: flat front light, a circular catchlight in every gloss. | Social-media and beauty looks. |

**Daylight**

| Studio | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Window Light | A big window to one side and a white bounce opposite; you'll see the window's panes in glossy surfaces. | Lifestyle and editorial. |
| Overcast Daylight | Soft, cool light from all over the sky and gentle shadows. | Outdoor gear, shoes, plants - anything that should feel real. |

**Specialist**

| Studio | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Gradient Chrome | Two tall graduated softboxes, a graded top light and black flags: long smooth highlights and defining dark lines. | Polished metal: watches, cutlery, appliances. |
| Glass: Bright Field | A bright panel behind and black flags either side: crisp dark outlines on every glass edge, glowing liquids. | Bottles, glasses, clear plastics. |
| Glass: Dark Field | Tall strips behind and one above outline glass in bright lines against black, with a hidden glow for liquids. | Premium spirits and fragrance on black. |
| Jewellery Sparkle | A soft tent for the metal, rims, and a scatter of tiny hard lights that make stones flash; the camera flagged black. | Rings, gems, polished small metal. |

**Dramatic**

| Studio | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Hero Gradient | A dark set with a soft glow behind, a sculpting key and two strip lights on the edges, semi-gloss floor. | The premium launch hero shot. |
| Low-Key Glossy | A long overhead strip and two edge strips mirrored in a black glass floor. | Watches, phones, premium electronics. |
| Rim Silhouette | Black on black, drawn only by two strong rims and one above. | Dark products: headphones, cameras, sneakers. |
| Spotlight | One hard light from almost straight above and a pool of light behind; everything else falls to black. | Bold, graphic, theatrical. |
| Chiaroscuro | One large soft light from the side, black on the other, a faint kick on the far edge. | Shapes, textures, food, still life. |
| Backlit Halo | A glowing halo straight behind throws the product into silhouette with light wrapping every edge; it lights glass from behind too. | The dramatic reveal. |

**Colour**

| Studio | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Cinematic Teal & Orange | A cool teal key against a warm orange back light on a deep teal set. | Gadgets, gear, sneakers, bottles. |
| Split Gel | Two coloured gels, one each side, meeting on the product (magenta and cyan, or your own). | Bold, graphic colour. |
| Hard Sun Pop | Hard sunlight on a coloured seamless: crisp graphic shadows, punchy colour. | Trendy brand shots - pick your colour with Backdrop: Custom colour. |

## Good to know

- Glass with liquid inside (a perfume, a filled bottle) looks right in
  Tequila's own render engine rather than Cycles, which has no medium
  priority.
- The studios are made of environment lights, so they show in reflections
  exactly like real softboxes and strips. A studio replaces your
  environment while it's on (except with Backdrop: My environment); **Back
  to my look** brings yours back.
- The plugin keeps a black 2048 × 1024 environment image (8 MB) in its data
  folder to paint the studios on, so small lights stay sharp whatever image
  your scene had.
